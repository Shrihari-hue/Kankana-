    What clients can book

    Venues & Pricing

    Changes apply to new quotations immediately. Existing quotations keep their prices. Untick “Live” to hide an item from the website without deleting it.

    Venues & halls

    Booked by the day for celebrations. “Type” shows on the booking page (e.g. Event venue, Hall, Open-air).

    Villas & rooms

    Booked by the night. “Units” is how many you have of each (e.g. 6 villas). Leave GST blank to use the default rate.

    Turf, courts & café

    Booked by the hour. “Charged” per hour (courts) or per guest (café tables — units = seats, sitting = hours a table is held). Hours use 24-hour time (6 = 6 AM, 22 = 10 PM).
