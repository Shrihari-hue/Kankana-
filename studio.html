How We Think About This

We didn't want a page of headshots and job titles. What Kankana makes — a venue, a stay, a wedding — already says more about the people behind it than a bio ever could. So instead, we introduce them through their craft.

Founder

Started with one wedding, and a refusal to compromise

Kankana began with a single wedding in Ubud. What's grown around it since — themed venues, curated stays, and full-scale celebrations across Bali and Myanmar — still runs on the same instinct: build slowly, source locally, and never let scale dull the detail.

— Founder & Creative Director
